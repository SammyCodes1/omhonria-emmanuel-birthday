A Man After God's Heart

Pastor Omhonria Emmanuel has given himself to the Word, to prayer, and to the people God has placed in his care. In the pulpit and in quiet rooms, he shepherds with patience, clarity, and a steady love for souls.

As set man of Psalmist Nation Tabernacle, he calls this house to PURITY, POWER, AND PRECISION. His teaching raises God-yielded men and women on the 7 mountains of influence and raising Kingdom minded people.

Today we honour the faith that keeps him, the service that marks him, and the leadership that has shaped this congregation. May the Lord continue to strengthen the work of his hands.

The Journey

  1. Answered the Call

    He yielded to the voice of God and set his life apart for the gospel, with a heart ready to shepherd.

  2. Founded the Church

    Psalmist Nation Tabernacle was raised as a house of prayer, purity, and the Word, a place for God-yielded lives.

  3. A Teaching Mandate

    From the pulpit he has opened Scripture with clarity and fire, calling men and women to walk in truth.

  4. Church Expansion

    The work grew as families were gathered, disciples were sent, and the house made room for more.

  5. Community Outreach

    The church went beyond the walls to serve, pray, and lift the city with the love of Christ.
